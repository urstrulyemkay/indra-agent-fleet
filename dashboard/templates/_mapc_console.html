What is "delivery_delayed"?

The recipient's mail server accepted the email but hasn't confirmed inbox delivery yet — usually because their server is busy or temporarily unreachable (called "greylisting"). Resend retries automatically for up to 72 hours. No action needed; it almost always resolves to "delivered" on its own. If it stays delayed beyond 3 days, the subscriber's email provider may be blocking bulk senders.
