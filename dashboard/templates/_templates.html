How activation works

Click Activate on a template → it joins the active rotation for that (category, niche, kind). Indra immediately publishes a JSON array of all active templates for that combo to Langfuse under auto_send.<category>.<niche>.<kind>. n8n's auto-responder fetches that prompt at send time and picks one variant at random. Deactivate or delete: the active set is recomputed and Langfuse is updated within the same call.
