{% elif test_id == "attachment-style" %}

What this measures. Attachment style is the template you learned for closeness in your earliest relationships. The two axes are anxiety (how often you worry about being abandoned) and avoidance (how often you pull away when intimacy increases). Where you sit on those axes puts you into one of four styles. It's a pattern, not a verdict.

What's inside the full report:

  • Your dominant style with the specific behaviors it produces in adult relationships
  • Your default move when conflict shows up, and what it costs you
  • One growth direction toward earned-secure attachment, with a concrete first step
  • A short "what to do when" guide for the recurring patterns of your style
{% elif test_id == "career-interest" %}

What this measures. Holland Code (RIASEC) describes the work environments and tasks that naturally pull you in. It's not a verdict on what you should do for a living. It's a description of your gravity, useful for noticing whether your current role matches the way your attention actually wants to move.

What's inside the full report:

  • Your top three interest codes and what they typically combine into
  • Sample career clusters and role descriptions that match your three-letter code
  • One adjacent code worth exploring if you want to stretch beyond your default
  • A reflection prompt for whether your current role matches your code, and what to do if not

What this measures. Cognitive Snapshot uses ICAR-style items to map where your reasoning is sharpest right now. Cognitive abilities are levers that can be trained. What's inside the full report:

  • Your strongest sub-domain and what it predicts in real-world reasoning

  • How your overall pattern compares to a typical adult sample
  • A note on cognitive flexibility, because no single snapshot tells the whole story
